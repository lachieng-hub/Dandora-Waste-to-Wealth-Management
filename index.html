The challenge

A growing problem.
A lot left unused.

In Dandora, discarded materials are part of everyday life. Too often, they end up in the wrong places, while the people closest to the problem have too few resources to shape what happens next.

↘

Plastic, organic matter and other recoverable materials are treated as one burden instead of different resources with different futures.

There is another way to look at it →
